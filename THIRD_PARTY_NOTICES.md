## TKA Platform elemental relationship model

SpiroAnim's elemental relationship labels adapt the elemental classification documented in the
TKA Platform VTG domain model:

- Earth, Water, Air, and Fire map to Together-Same, Split-Same, Together-Opposite, and
  Split-Opposite. TKA Platform records that these four mappings were popularized by Leonardo Icaza
  (@poidanceflow) and taught in video form by Ronan McLoughlin.
- Sun and Moon map to Quarter-Same and Quarter-Opposite. These quarter-time extensions were
  introduced by Austen Cloud as part of TKA Platform's six-element model.

The elemental classification is a separate community overlay on VTG, not part of VTG itself.

Source: [TKA Platform `elemental-model.ts`](https://github.com/austencloud/tka-platform/blob/48a683ab7654ed461016126928e1be35e40209f2/packages/vtg-domain/src/data/elemental-model.ts)
